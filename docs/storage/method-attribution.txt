== decisions taken against the design document ==

1. initialize() is retained as a no-op rather than deleted. The design asks for
   its removal, but tests/unit/test_migration_baseline.py asserts it exists
   exactly once and that its body is a docstring plus a return. Deleting it
   would mean weakening a test to land a refactor, which AGENTS.md forbids. Its
   disposition belongs in its own commit, where deleting the test is the subject
   rather than the cost.

2. _THREAD_SANDBOX_SOURCE lives in _sql/rows.py, not _sql/sandboxes.py as the
   design sketched. The shared get_job projection in _sql/getters.py needs it,
   and placing it with the sandbox writes would make the two modules import each
   other.

3. get_job becomes _sql/getters.py:get_job_projection(conn, job_id), taking the
   caller's connection like every other helper in that package. The conversation
   read calls it inside its own transaction; finalize_session_fork reads it after
   its transaction committed, through _read_after_commit, which opens exactly one
   connection from the pool that repository already holds. Neither repository
   depends on the other, which is what the design asked for.

4. mypy is scoped to the new boundary rather than to all of cloud_agent. Turning
   it on over code that never had a checker reports 190 findings in 34 files, and
   the ignore list that would silence them is worse than no checker. Modules join
   the scope as their callers are narrowed; store.py is excluded outright because
   it is deleted at the end of this migration.
